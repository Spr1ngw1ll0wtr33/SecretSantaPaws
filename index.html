Secret Santa Paws

The gift exchange organiser

🔒︎ Your spreadsheet stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1 Load the spreadsheet

Choose the participants spreadsheet (.xlsx). It needs these headings in the first row: IG Account, Dog's Name, Address, Country of Residence, Will Send To, Email Address.

Which countries and choices does the app understand?

2 Make the matches

Everyone sends one gift and receives one, nobody gets themselves, and everyone's Will Send To rule is followed. Two people only send to each other when there's no other way.

3 Master list

Download this before sending anything. It's your back-up if any emails go astray, and you can reload it in step 1 to carry on later.

4 The email

Edit the wording here (for example, to fill in the spending limits). These markers are swapped for each person's match: <Dog Name(s)> <IG Account> <Address> <Email Address>

5 Send the emails

For each person, press a send button, check the email, press Send in your email, then tick Sent here. Ticks are remembered on this device.

Show these buttons:

Gmail and Outlook.com open in a new browser tab, so you need to be logged in to them. "Email app" opens the email program on this device. "Copy" copies the address, subject and message so you can paste them anywhere.

Only needed if more than one Google account is signed in on this browser. It makes the Gmail button open the right one. Always check the “From” line before pressing Send.
