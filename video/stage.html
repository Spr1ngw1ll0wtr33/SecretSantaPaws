How the app works

A calm walk-through. Pause at any time.

The whole job is 5 steps, from top to bottom of the page

  1. Load the spreadsheet
  2. Make the matches
  3. Download the master list (your back-up)
  4. Check the email wording
  5. Send each email, and tick it off

🔒︎ Everything happens on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded.

Before you start, you need:

  1. The final spreadsheet, downloaded as an Excel file (.xlsx) to your Mac, with the list on the first tab
  2. To be logged in to Gmail in your web browser
  3. Plenty of time – but you can stop and carry on later

In this video we use the made-up test spreadsheet.

Recap

  1. Load the spreadsheet – look for the green box
  2. Make the matches – look for the four green ticks
  3. Download the master list – before sending anything
  4. Check the wording – fill in the spending limits
  5. For each card: Gmail → check → Send → tick Sent. Save progress every 10.

If anything looks wrong, stop and ask the app builder before sending. Nothing is sent until you press Send in Gmail.
