SECRET SANTA PAWS - TRIAL RUN
=============================
Version 3, 26/10/2026

The app is a web page. Your spreadsheet is read on your own device:
it is never uploaded anywhere, and no email is sent unless you press
Send yourself. The test spreadsheet has 78 made-up entries from 72
made-up accounts (some accounts have entered two dogs). The email
addresses end in @example.com, which don't belong to anyone.


PART A - OPEN THE APP AND GET THE TEST SPREADSHEET
--------------------------------------------------
 1. Open this address in your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox
    or Edge), on a computer, phone or tablet:

      https://spr1ngw1ll0wtr33.github.io/SecretSantaPaws/

    You should see the dog picture, "Secret Santa Paws", and step 1
    "Load the spreadsheet". It's worth bookmarking.
    (On a phone, don't use "Install" or "Add to Home screen" -
    it's a web page, not an app to install. A bookmark is enough.)

 2. Download the made-up test spreadsheet by opening this link:

      https://github.com/Spr1ngw1ll0wtr33/SecretSantaPaws/raw/main/test-dummy.xlsx

    It goes into your Downloads folder.

(Alternative without the internet: if you have the zip file
"Secret-Santa-Paws-trial", unpack it - right-click and "Extract Here"
on Linux, or double-click on a Mac - and double-click "index.html".
The test spreadsheet is in the same folder.)


PART B - WHICH SEND BUTTON TO USE
---------------------------------
In step 5 each person has send buttons. Which one works depends on
the device:

  ON A COMPUTER (Mac, Windows or Linux):  use "Gmail".
    It opens Gmail in a browser tab with the email filled in.
    You need to be logged in to Gmail in that browser.

  ON A PHONE OR TABLET:  use "Email app".
    It opens the Gmail app with the email filled in.
    (The "Gmail" button does NOT work on a phone - it just shows
    your inbox, without the email.)

The app ticks the right button for the device it's on. You can
change which buttons show with the tick boxes under "Show these
buttons". "Copy" works everywhere as a fall-back: it copies the
address, subject and message so you can paste them into any email.

ALWAYS skim the email before pressing Send, to check the amounts,
the dog's name and the address are right.


PART C - TRY IT
---------------
 3. Step 1: tap or click "Choose spreadsheet" and pick
    "test-dummy.xlsx" from your Downloads.
    You should see a green box: "78 entries from 72 accounts".
 4. Click "Which countries and choices does the app understand?"
    to see the country list.
 5. Step 2: click "Make the matches".
    You should see a green "Checked and correct" box with 4 ticks.
    You should ALSO see an amber box, because the test sheet has
    been set up to include two awkward situations on purpose:
      - three Canada accounts that will only send within Canada,
        two of them with two dogs each. One of those accounts has
        to send to the same account twice, and two of them end up
        as each other's Secret Santa. There's no way round it.
      - the two Iceland accounts both say "Iceland only", so they
        have to be each other's Secret Santa.
 6. Step 3: click "Download master list". A spreadsheet called
    "Secret-Santa-Paws-master-list-(date)-(time).xlsx" goes into
    your Downloads folder. Open it to look at it.
 7. Step 4: look at the email wording and the preview. It should
    start "Dear (dog's name)," and the subject line should include
    the dog's name too. Try changing "£TBA" to an amount. The
    preview should change as well.
 8. Step 5: tick "Sent" for 10 people. The counter and green bar
    should move, and after the 10th tick an amber "Time to save
    your progress" box appears.
 9. Click "Save progress". A new master list downloads (with the
    ticks in its Sent column) and the amber box disappears.
10. SEND BUTTON (safe): press the send button for any person -
    "Gmail" on a computer, "Email app" on a phone (see Part B).
    A new email opens with the message filled in. Check your
    changed amount from step 7 is in it. DON'T press Send (the
    address is made up) - discard the draft instead.
    On a computer, press "Gmail" for a second person: it should
    reuse the same browser tab.

Optional extra tests (easiest on a computer):
11. RELOAD TEST: at the bottom, click "Clear everything saved on
    this device" and confirm. Then in step 1 click "Reload a saved
    master list" and choose the master list from step 9. The same
    matches should come back, with your 10 ticks.
12. ERROR TEST: open test-dummy.xlsx in your spreadsheet program,
    make a deliberate mistake (e.g. type "Untied Kingdom" as a
    country, delete an email address, or give one of a two-dog
    account's rows a different Will Send To), use "Save As" to save
    it under a new name as an .xlsx file, then load that file in
    step 1. You should get a red box naming the row.
13. IMPOSSIBLE TEST: open test-dummy.xlsx in your spreadsheet
    program. Open Find & Replace (Ctrl+H on Linux, Cmd+F then
    "Replace" in Numbers on a Mac). Find "Any", replace with "UK",
    and switch ON the option "Entire cells" (in LibreOffice it's
    under "Other options"; in Numbers choose "Whole Words" and
    "Match Case"). Otherwise "Germany" becomes "GermUK"!
    Replace all, "Save As" a new .xlsx file, load it in step 1 and
    press "Make the matches". A red box should say how many
    problems there are, with "Show who is involved" links.


PART D - FEEDBACK
-----------------
Please note, step by step:
  - anything that didn't happen as described above
    (a screenshot helps)
  - anything confusing or badly worded
  - the computer or phone, and the browser, you used

To clear the saved test draw when you've finished, use the "Clear
everything saved on this device" button at the bottom of the app.

NOTE: if the app doesn't remember your ticks after closing the
browser, your browser may be set to delete site data when it
closes. That's why "Save progress" exists: the downloaded master
list is always your back-up.
