SECRET SANTA PAWS - TRIAL RUN
=============================
Version 2.1, 26/10/2026

Everything here runs on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded or
published, and no email is sent unless you press Send inside Gmail.
The test spreadsheet has 78 made-up entries from 72 made-up accounts
(some accounts have entered two dogs). The email addresses end in
@example.com, which don't belong to anyone.


PART A - UNPACK THE FILES (once)
--------------------------------
On a Linux PC (e.g. Zorin):
 1. Open the Files app and go to your Downloads folder.
 2. Right-click "Secret-Santa-Paws-trial.zip" and choose "Extract Here".

On an Apple Mac:
 1. Open Finder and go to your Downloads folder.
 2. Double-click "Secret-Santa-Paws-trial.zip". The Mac unpacks it.

Either way, a folder called "Secret-Santa-Paws-trial" appears. Open it.
You should see 6 files:
     index.html          - the app itself
     matching.js         - the matching rules
     xlsx.full.min.js    - reads and writes spreadsheets
     header.jpg          - the Secret Santa Paws picture
     test-dummy.xlsx     - the made-up test spreadsheet
     HOW-TO-TRY-IT.txt   - these instructions
Keep all of these files together in the same folder.


PART B - OPEN THE APP
---------------------
 3. Double-click "index.html". It opens in your web browser
    (Firefox on Zorin, Safari on a Mac, unless you've changed it).
    If it opens in something else, right-click it, choose
    "Open With" and pick your web browser.
 4. You should see the dog picture, "Secret Santa Paws", and
    step 1 "Load the spreadsheet".


PART C - TRY IT
---------------
 5. Step 1: click "Choose spreadsheet" and pick "test-dummy.xlsx"
    from the Secret-Santa-Paws-trial folder.
    You should see a green box: "78 entries from 72 accounts".
 6. Click "Which countries and choices does the app understand?"
    to see the country list.
 7. Step 2: click "Make the matches".
    You should see a green "Checked and correct" box with 4 ticks.
    You should ALSO see an amber box, because the test sheet has
    been set up to include two awkward situations on purpose:
      - three Canada accounts that will only send within Canada,
        two of them with two dogs each. One of those accounts has
        to send to the same account twice, and two of them end up
        as each other's Secret Santa. There's no way round it.
      - the two Iceland accounts both say "Iceland only", so they
        have to be each other's Secret Santa.
 8. Step 3: click "Download master list". A spreadsheet called
    "Secret-Santa-Paws-master-list-(date)-(time).xlsx" goes into
    your Downloads folder. Open it to look at it.
 9. Step 4: look at the email wording and the preview. It should
    start "Dear (dog's name)," and the subject line should include
    the dog's name too. Try changing "£TBA" to an amount. The
    preview should change as well.
10. Step 5: tick "Sent" for 10 people. The counter and green bar
    should move, and after the 10th tick an amber "Time to save
    your progress" box appears.
11. Click "Save progress". A new master list downloads (with the
    ticks in its Sent column) and the amber box disappears.

Optional extra tests:
12. GMAIL BUTTON (safe): click "Gmail" on any person. A Gmail
    "New message" window opens with the message filled in.
    DON'T press Send (the address is made up). Close the draft and
    delete it from Gmail's Drafts. Then click "Gmail" for a second
    person: it should reuse the same browser tab.
13. RELOAD TEST: at the bottom, click "Clear everything saved on
    this device" and confirm. Then in step 1 click "Reload a saved
    master list" and choose the master list from step 11. The same
    matches should come back, with your 10 ticks.
14. ERROR TEST: open test-dummy.xlsx in your spreadsheet program,
    make a deliberate mistake (e.g. type "Untied Kingdom" as a
    country, delete an email address, or give one of a two-dog
    account's rows a different Will Send To), use "Save As" to save
    it under a new name as an .xlsx file, then load that file in
    step 1. You should get a red box naming the row.
15. IMPOSSIBLE TEST: open test-dummy.xlsx in your spreadsheet
    program. Open Find & Replace (Ctrl+H on Linux, Cmd+F then
    "Replace" in Numbers on a Mac). Find "Any", replace with "UK",
    and switch ON the option "Entire cells" (in LibreOffice it's
    under "Other options"; in Numbers choose "Whole Words" and
    "Match Case"). Otherwise "Germany" becomes "GermUK"!
    Replace all, "Save As" a new .xlsx file, load it in step 1 and
    press "Make the matches". A red box should say how many
    problems there are, with "Show who is involved" links.


PART D - FEEDBACK
-----------------
Please note, step by step:
  - anything that didn't happen as described above
    (a screenshot helps)
  - anything confusing or badly worded
  - the computer and browser you used

When finished, you can delete the whole Secret-Santa-Paws-trial
folder. To clear the saved test draw first, use the "Clear
everything saved on this device" button at the bottom of the app.

NOTE: if the app doesn't remember your ticks after closing the
browser, your browser may be set to delete site data when it
closes. That's why "Save progress" exists: the downloaded master
list is always your back-up.
